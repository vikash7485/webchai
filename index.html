FAQ

Good questions

Anything else? Just ask, you'll get a straight answer.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work with businesses and agencies anywhere. Everything happens online: briefs, calls, progress previews and handover.

How is a project priced?

After a short discovery chat you get a fixed quote based on scope, so you know the full cost before work starts. Agencies get partner rates.

How does white-label work with my clients?

You manage the client, we build the site. Nothing carries the WebChai name, we never contact your client, and we're happy to sign an NDA.

Who owns the website when it's done?

You do (or your agency's client does). You get full access to the site, files and accounts at handover.

How do we communicate during the project?

However suits you best: WhatsApp, email or video calls. You talk directly to the developer, not an account manager.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We fix anything that isn't right after launch, and can look after updates and changes as your business grows.
